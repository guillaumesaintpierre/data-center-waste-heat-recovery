# References and data provenance

Accessed/checked: 17 November 2026. The links support *context, definitions and methodology*. They are **not** sources for the hypothetical values in `config.json`.

1. **IEA (2026).** *Key Questions on Energy and AI — Executive summary.* https://www.iea.org/reports/key-questions-on-energy-and-ai/executive-summary — global data-centre electricity demand (2025 and projected 2030); this is background, not model input.
2. **European Commission (2024).** Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1364, Annexes (PUE, ERF definitions). https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=OJ%3AL_202401364 — formal reporting boundaries. The screening model deliberately does **not** label heat delivery as certified ERF.
3. **ASHRAE.** *AI Data Center Energy Performance Framework: Energy and Thermal Efficiency.* https://www.ashrae.org/technical-resources/topics-and-initiatives/ai-data-center/energy-and-thermal-efficiency — efficiency measures, heat reuse considerations and system performance indicators.
4. **IEA (2025).** *Opportunities for district heating in the changing energy landscape.* https://www.iea.org/commentaries/opportunities-for-district-heating-in-the-changing-energy-landscape — waste heat sources and need for nearby viable heat demand.
5. **IEA DHC Annex TS2 (2021).** *Low Temperature District Heating Implementation Guidebook.* https://www.iea-dhc.org/fileadmin/documents/Annex_TS2/IEA_DHC_Annex_TS2_Transition_to_low_temperature_DH.pdf — temperature-lift and second-law efficiency modelling context. The current simulation uses a freely chosen COP efficiency value rather than calibrating to this guidebook.
6. **Energinet — Energi Data Service.** *API Guide.* https://www.energidataservice.dk/guides/api-guides — public market dataset access, time filters, field names, data usage rules. The bundled results use **synthetic** electricity prices, not Energinet historical measurements.
7. **Data catalogue (Energinet/Energi Data Service).** *Day-Ahead Prices.* https://datavejviser.dk/katalog/energidataservice/e759dc98-249b-4b1f-94ef-261754be05f8 — describes the market-price dataset. The optional importer supports 15-minute DayAheadPrices from October 2025 onward.

**Integrity rule:** A modelled quantity can be precise as the result of a calculation without being accurate as a prediction of a real plant. The distinction is essential throughout the report.

## Historical inputs: enabled, not yet retrieved in the packaged project

- **DMI (Danish Meteorological Institute)**, station observations `temp_mean_past1h` via the [meteorological observations API](https://www.dmi.dk/friedata/dokumentation/meteorological-observation-api). Weather source: © DMI, [CC BY 4.0](https://www.dmi.dk/friedata/dokumentation/terms-of-use). The importer aligns hour-ending measurements to starting UTC hour and flags minor imputation. No weather data is embedded in the package until imported.
- **Energinet, Energi Data Service**, `Elspotprices` and `DayAheadPrices`, DK2 bidding zone, EUR/MWh. Market-data licence and credit: Energinet, [terms](https://www.energidataservice.dk/terms-and-conditions). Historical series must be actually downloaded to generate historical outputs. See [OBSERVED_DATA_2025](OBSERVED_DATA_2025.md).
